# WinPure® — Data Quality & Entity Resolution Software

**Build trusted, AI-ready data with powerful data profiling, cleansing, matching, deduplication and entity resolution.**

WinPure® provides data quality and data matching software that helps organisations identify, clean, standardise and connect information across multiple data sources.

Our technology combines **data profiling, data cleansing, fuzzy matching, entity resolution and Golden Record creation** in a secure, no-code environment — helping organisations turn fragmented and inconsistent information into trusted data.

## 🔍 Data Matching & Entity Resolution

WinPure helps organisations identify duplicate and related records even when data is incomplete, inconsistent or incorrectly formatted.

Matching capabilities include:

* Exact and fuzzy data matching
* Phonetic matching
* Multi-column matching
* Entity resolution
* Duplicate detection and data deduplication
* Configurable matching algorithms and rules
* Explainable matching results
* Master Record selection
* Golden Record creation

## 🧹 Data Quality & Data Cleansing

Understand and improve your data before matching, migration, analytics or AI.

WinPure provides:

* Data profiling and quality analysis
* Completeness and validity analysis
* Data standardisation
* Data cleansing and transformation
* Pattern and frequency analysis
* Regular expression (REGEX) cleansing
* Data quality insights and recommendations

## 🏆 Trusted Golden Records

WinPure helps consolidate duplicate and related records into trusted Master and Golden Records, providing a more reliable view of customers, citizens, suppliers and other entities.

## 🔐 Secure by Design

WinPure Clean & Match can operate within your own environment, helping organisations maintain control over sensitive data while performing advanced data quality and entity resolution processes.
